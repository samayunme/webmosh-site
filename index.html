05 Questions

The things founders ask first

If yours isn’t here, ask it on the call — we’d rather answer it before you pay than after.

01 I don’t live in the UK or the US. Can I still own the company?

Yes. Neither a UK Ltd nor a US LLC requires you to be a resident or a citizen. You will need a registered office in the UK or a registered agent in your US state, valid ID, and a compliant address for correspondence — we provide the first two and check the third.

Non-resident owners do have extra reporting duties, most notably Form 5472 for a foreign-owned US LLC. We put those on your filing calendar from day one rather than letting you discover them a year later.

02 How fast can the company be formed?

A UK Ltd is usually incorporated the same day or within 24 hours of Companies House accepting the application. US timelines depend on the state: Delaware and Wyoming are typically a few business days, while some states take up to two weeks. EIN issuance is separate and depends on the IRS.

We quote the realistic range for your state before you pay, not the best case.

03 Can you open the business bank account for me?

We prepare every document a bank or payment provider asks for — certificate of incorporation, governing documents, proof of address, ownership structure — and we point you to the providers that actually accept founders in your situation.

The approval itself is always the bank’s decision. Anyone promising you a guaranteed account is telling you something they cannot control.

04 Who owns the website and the hosting?

You do. The domain is registered in your company’s name, you hold administrator access, and everything is exportable. If you ever leave, you leave with the site, the content and the analytics history.

05 Do you actually file the taxes, or just prepare them?

Both, depending on the filing. Bookkeeping, VAT returns, confirmation statements and routine submissions we handle directly. Statutory accounts and US federal returns are prepared and filed with licensed accountants in the relevant jurisdiction, so the signature on your return belongs to someone qualified to give it.

06 Can I take one service instead of everything?

Yes. Plenty of clients start with just a website, or just the formation, and add the rest when they need it. The bundle is cheaper and less work for you, but it is not a condition.

07 What does it cost?

Formation is a fixed fee plus the government filing fee, quoted up front. Websites are fixed-scope projects. Hosting, care, marketing and compliance run monthly. You get one written quote covering all of it before any work starts, and we don’t begin until you have approved the number.
