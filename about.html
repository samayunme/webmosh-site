How we work

Four commitments we would rather be held to than explain away

None of these are hard to do. They are just the first things to go when a business is spread across five suppliers who each own one piece of it.

Hold us to any of them. All four are visible in the packages and in the proposal before you have paid anything.

  1. 01

    Fixed fees, quoted before we start

    One written proposal covering formation, build and the monthly work. No hourly drift, and nothing begins until you have approved the figure.

    Government charges are passed through at cost and named separately.

  2. 02

    You own everything we build

    The domain is registered to your company, you hold administrator access, and the site, content and analytics history are exportable.

    If you ever leave, you leave with all of it.

  3. 03

    One contact, answerable for the result

    Not a ticket queue and not a handoff between departments. The same people who formed the company know what the website does and when the next filing is due.

    One monthly report, in plain numbers.

  4. 04

    Filings tracked, not remembered

    VAT quarters, Corporation Tax, confirmation statements, Form 5472 and franchise tax sit on a shared calendar from the day the company exists.

    Statutory work is signed off by licensed accountants in each jurisdiction.

Where we operate

Two jurisdictions, handled end to end

We work with founders wherever they live. Neither structure asks you to be a resident or a citizen. What they ask for is an address, an agent, and somebody keeping track of the dates.

United Kingdom

Incorporated through Companies House, usually the same day or within 24 hours of the application being accepted.

  • Registered office and directors service address
  • PSC register and web authentication code
  • UTR, VAT registration and Corporation Tax
  • Confirmation statement and annual accounts

United States

LLCs and C-Corps in Delaware, Wyoming or your state of choice. Timelines vary by state, so we quote the range you should actually plan around rather than the best case.

  • Registered agent and business address
  • EIN, and ITIN support where it is needed
  • Articles of Organization and operating agreement
  • Form 1120, Form 5472 and franchise tax

WEBMOSH is not a law firm or a licensed tax adviser. Statutory accounts and tax returns are prepared and filed with licensed accountants in the relevant jurisdiction.
